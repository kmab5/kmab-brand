# Brand voice and messaging

## One-line positioning

**Engineer, mathematician, and game-builder who turns hard ideas into fast, useful artifacts.**

## Primary tagline

**Hard ideas, made usable.**

## Brand narrative

Sami learned to notice before he learned to build: pages turning, machines coming apart, patterns in mathematics, differences between people and places. That instinct became a way of working. Sit with the difficult thing. Understand its parts. Remove what is unnecessary. Build the smallest clear system that works.

The brand has two signatures. **sami** is the human voice: reflective, warm, exact, and open. **kmab** is the maker handle: technical, compressed, experimental, and performance-minded. The lambda connects them. It is mathematics, code, transformation, and a path that changes meaning when viewed from another angle.

## Core values

1. **Clarity before cleverness** — complexity may be necessary; confusion is not.
2. **Curiosity with follow-through** — take things apart, then make something useful from what was learned.
3. **Persistence without theater** — keep moving until the problem gives.
4. **Human range** — design for different languages, contexts, abilities, and ways of thinking.
5. **Play with purpose** — games, humor, and side quests are valid forms of serious learning.

## Personality

Precise, observant, quietly ambitious, intellectually restless, grounded, and slightly playful.

## Voice rules

- Begin with a concrete image, fact, or problem.
- Use short declarative sentences around important ideas.
- Explain technical work in plain language.
- Prefer evidence and outcomes to self-praise.
- Use self-aware humor sparingly.
- Allow a small aside or unexpected line when it makes the writing more human.
- Keep racing language as seasoning, never the whole meal.

## Avoid

Corporate filler, inflated certainty, generic inspiration, performative humility, unexplained jargon, excessive exclamation marks, and metaphors that do not survive literal reading.

## Message examples

**Portfolio intro:** I build clean, fast interfaces and small, elegant systems — and the occasional browser game.

**Project description:** A browser rendition of Gebeta with a rules engine, turn logic, and just enough AI to make losing interesting.

**Call to action:** Bring the hard problem. We will make it usable.
